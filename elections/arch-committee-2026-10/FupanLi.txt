Background:

I have been a maintainer and active contributor to the Kata Containers community since 2018. 
Over the years, I developed the Kata shimv2 to streamline the architecture into a two-process 
model, significantly boosting its adoption in the Kubernetes ecosystem. I also authored the 
Rust-based kata-agent that helped transition Kata from 1.0 to 2.0, and served as the architect 
and key developer of runtime-rs.

Since being elected as a Kata AC member last year, I have continued to drive the project forward. 
As the maintainer of Dragonball, the inline VMM built into Kata, I have actively expanded its 
capabilities, including driving support for Intel TDX. Building on this momentum, I successfully 
promoted runtime-rs to become the default runtime—integrating our ongoing Confidential Computing (CoCo) 
efforts—which directly facilitated the release of Kata 4.0 today, October 8, 2026.

Professionally, I am a Senior Engineer at Ant Group, where I maintain the world's largest Kata Containers 
cluster. Currently, I am also developing and maintaining Ant Group's AI agent sandbox system, actively 
working to integrate Kata Containers into this infrastructure to provide secure, isolated environments 
for AI workloads. My extensive experience bridging large-scale production deployments with upstream 
community development equips me to make highly practical, impactful decisions. If re-elected, I will 
continue dedicating myself to advancing Kata’s architecture, security, and broader industry adoption.
